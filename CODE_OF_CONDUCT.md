# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

- Demonstrating empathy, technical rigor, and constructive feedback.
- Respecting differing viewpoints and architectural trade-offs.
- Focusing on what is best for security, reliability, and the community.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to **[Encrypted Security Dispatch](https://rasaxmachina.com/collins#encrypted-dispatch)**. All complaints will be reviewed promptly and fairly.
